SYSTEM PROTOCOLS

Clear answers regarding deployment, infrastructure, and performance mechanics.

Clear answers regarding deployment, infrastructure, and performance mechanics.

How are the training plans delivered?

Instantly upon validation. You will receive an encrypted digital link granting immediate access to your performance dashboard, mobile tracking matrices, and video breakdown library. Zero processing delays.

Is this framework suitable for beginners?

ARES is engineered for high-performance execution. While our plans scale in intensity based on your initial baseline data, a foundational understanding of compound movements (squat, bench, deadlift) is highly recommended for safety and optimal neuromuscular adaptation.

Do I need access to a fully equipped commercial gym?

Yes. Our strategic programming relies heavily on mechanical tension and progressive overload protocols. Access to free weights, barbells, heavy dumbbells, and standard cable infrastructure is mandatory to complete the cycles.

What is the cancellation and refund architecture?

Because our system grants immediate, irreversible access to proprietary digital training intellectual property, all sales are final. You can manage or terminate your subscription cycle at any time with a single click through your profile node.

Does the system include personalized nutrition plans?

ARES provides targeted macro-nutrient frameworks and metabolic fueling protocols engineered to support elite recovery. We do not offer generic, restrictive meal plans. Instead, we deploy mathematical nutritional guidelines tailored precisely to power your specific performance vector.

Will I receive future updates to the training matrix?

Absolutely. The athletic landscape evolves, and so do our protocols. All active subscription profiles receive seamless, automated access updates to their dashboard whenever new high-performance training blocks, phase metrics, or recovery methodologies are deployed to the system.
